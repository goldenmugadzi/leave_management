1. Role Overview & Responsibilities

Your Role: As a Team Member, you support fault location activities under team leader guidance and provide essential field assistance.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Assist with fault location equipment setup
  • Follow team leader instructions
  • Maintain safety compliance at all times
  • Report findings and observations
  • Update work progress when directed
  • Support team coordination activities

What You Can Do:

  • ✅ View basic fault information for your team's work
  • ✅ Update your personal work status
  • ✅ Report safety concerns
  • ✅ Access mobile-friendly system views
  • ✅ Communicate with team leader
  • ❌ Cannot modify fault assignments
  • ❌ Cannot access other teams' information
  • ❌ Cannot make system-wide changes
Remember: Your primary focus is supporting your team leader and maintaining safety standards. When in doubt, always ask your team leader for guidance.

3. Understanding Your Assignments

Work Assignment Information:

What You'll See:
  • Current Location: Where your team is working today
  • Fault Priority: How urgent the work is
  • Your Role: Specific tasks assigned to you
  • Safety Requirements: PPE and safety measures needed
  • Estimated Duration: How long the work should take

Priority Levels Explained:

VVIP (Very Very Important Person):
  • Highest priority - drop everything else
  • Often involves hospitals, schools, or essential services
  • Requires immediate response
Critical (Level 4):
  • High voltage or major customer impact
  • Extra safety precautions required
  • Fast response needed
High to Low (Levels 3-1):
  • Work assigned based on impact level
  • Follow team leader's scheduling
  • Normal safety procedures apply

Understanding Your Tasks:

Common Team Member Tasks:
  • Equipment Setup: Help set up fault locator devices
  • Safety Watch: Monitor for safety hazards
  • Site Preparation: Clear access paths, set up barriers
  • Data Recording: Write down readings and observations
  • Tool Management: Organize and maintain equipment
  • Communication: Relay information between team members

4. Field Work Procedures

Preparing for Field Work:

Before Leaving Base:
  1. Check your assigned tasks with team leader
  2. Verify you have correct PPE for the job
  3. Ensure your mobile device is charged
  4. Understand the work location and access requirements
  5. Review safety requirements for the voltage level

Safety First - Always:

  • PPE: Wear appropriate personal protective equipment
  • Team Work: Never work alone on electrical systems
  • Communication: Stay in contact with team leader
  • Procedures: Follow all safety procedures
  • Questions: Ask if you're unsure about anything

At the Work Site:

Site Arrival Checklist:
  1. Check in with team leader
  2. Assess site safety conditions
  3. Set up any required safety barriers
  4. Prepare work area as directed
  5. Assist with equipment setup

During Fault Location Work:

Your Support Role:
  • Follow team leader instructions precisely
  • Maintain constant safety awareness
  • Record data and readings as requested
  • Monitor equipment operation
  • Report any unusual observations immediately
  • Keep work area organized and safe
Important: If you notice anything unsafe or unusual, speak up immediately. Your safety observations are valuable to the team.

5. Providing Status Updates

When to Update the System:

Update Triggers:
  • When team leader asks you to
  • When you complete assigned tasks
  • If you encounter problems or delays
  • When moving to a new location
  • At the end of your work day

Simple Update Process:

Step 1: Open the mobile system
Step 2: Select Quick Update
Step 3: Choose your status:
  • Working: Currently performing assigned tasks
  • Complete: Finished assigned work
  • Need Help: Requiring assistance
  • Break: On scheduled break
Step 4: Add brief notes if needed
Step 5: Submit update

What to Include in Updates:

Good Update Notes:
  • "Equipment setup complete"
  • "Safety barriers in place"
  • "Readings recorded as requested"
  • "Task completed, awaiting next assignment"
  • "Need assistance with heavy equipment"
Keep Updates Simple: Brief, clear information is best. Your team leader handles detailed technical updates.

6. Safety Guidelines

Electrical Safety Basics:

  • Assume Live: Treat all electrical equipment as energized
  • PPE Required: Always wear appropriate protection
  • Minimum Distance: Maintain safe distances from equipment
  • No Shortcuts: Follow all safety procedures completely
  • Team Safety: Look out for your teammates

Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):

Standard PPE Requirements:
  • Safety helmet/hard hat
  • Safety glasses or face shield
  • Insulated gloves (voltage appropriate)
  • Safety boots
  • High-visibility clothing
  • Arc flash suit (if required)

Voltage-Specific Safety:

Low Voltage (0.4kV):
  • Standard electrical PPE
  • Basic safety procedures
  • Normal working distances
Medium Voltage (11kV-33kV):
  • Enhanced PPE requirements
  • Increased safety distances
  • Additional precautions
High Voltage (66kV+):
  • Maximum protection required
  • Extended safety distances
  • Special training requirements
Emergency Response:
  • Know emergency contact numbers
  • Understand first aid procedures
  • Practice emergency evacuation
  • Report incidents immediately

7. Communication & Support

Communication Hierarchy:

Your Primary Contact: Team Leader
  • First point of contact for questions
  • Provides daily work instructions
  • Handles technical issues
  • Coordinates with depot management
When Team Leader Is Unavailable:
  • Contact Depot Foreperson
  • Use emergency contact procedures
  • Report through system if possible

Effective Communication:

Clear Communication Tips:
  • Be specific about your location
  • Describe what you're doing
  • Report any concerns immediately
  • Confirm understanding of instructions
  • Ask questions if anything is unclear

Getting Help:

When to Ask for Help:
  • Unsure about safety procedures
  • Equipment not working properly
  • Uncomfortable with assigned task
  • Personal safety concerns
  • Need clarification on instructions
Remember: It's always better to ask questions than to guess. Your team leader wants you to be safe and successful.

8. Common Issues & Solutions

Mobile System Issues:

Issue: Can't log into the system on mobile
Solution:
  • Check your internet connection
  • Verify you're using the correct login credentials
  • Try refreshing the page
  • Contact team leader if problem persists
Issue: System running slowly on mobile device
Solution:
  • Close other apps to free up memory
  • Switch between WiFi and cellular data
  • Move to area with better signal strength
  • Use simple view options when available

Work-Related Issues:

Issue: Don't understand assigned task
Solution:
  • Ask team leader for clarification immediately
  • Request demonstration if needed
  • Don't proceed if unsure about safety
Issue: Equipment not working properly
Solution:
  • Stop using the equipment immediately
  • Report to team leader
  • Do not attempt repairs unless qualified
  • Document what happened
Issue: Safety concern at work site
Solution:
  • Stop work immediately
  • Alert team leader and other team members
  • Move to safe location if necessary
  • Report through proper channels
