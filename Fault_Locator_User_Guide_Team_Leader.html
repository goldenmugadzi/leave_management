1. Role Overview & Team Leadership

Your Leadership Role: As a Team Leader, you're responsible for coordinating your team's fault location activities and ensuring quality completion of assignments.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Lead fault location field operations
  • Coordinate team member activities
  • Report fault location progress and completion
  • Ensure safety compliance in the field
  • Communicate with depot management
  • Verify fault clearance and restoration

Access Permissions:

  • ✅ View your team's assigned faults
  • ✅ Update fault status and progress
  • ✅ Add field notes and location details
  • ✅ Request assistance from management
  • ✅ Access mobile-friendly field updates
  • ✅ View team assignment history
  • ❌ Cannot assign faults to other teams
  • ❌ Cannot access other teams' assignments

3. Managing Team Assignments

Viewing Active Assignments:

Assignment Overview:
  1. Access
    Simple Fault List
  2. Review all faults assigned to your team
  3. Check priority levels and deadlines
  4. View depot locations and details
  5. Read assignment notes from foreperson

Priority Management:

Understanding Priorities:
  • VVIP Faults: Absolute priority - handle immediately
  • Critical (Level 4): High voltage or major customer impact
  • High (Level 3): Significant customer impact
  • Medium (Level 2): Moderate impact
  • Low (Level 1): Minimal impact
Priority Guidelines:
  • Always address VVIP and Critical faults first
  • Consider travel efficiency when planning routes
  • High voltage faults require additional safety measures
  • Multiple low-priority faults in one area can be efficient

Assignment Planning:

Efficient Route Planning:
  1. Review all assigned fault locations
  2. Group geographically close faults
  3. Plan route considering priority levels
  4. Account for travel time between locations
  5. Communicate plan to team members

4. Field Operations

Pre-Field Preparation:

Before Leaving Base:
  1. Verify fault locator device is functional
  2. Check all safety equipment
  3. Review fault technical details:
    • Voltage level
    • Number of customers affected
    • Backfeed availability
    • Depot-specific instructions
  4. Ensure mobile device has system access
  5. Brief team on day's assignments
Safety First:
  • Always follow electrical safety procedures
  • Verify proper PPE for voltage level
  • Confirm system isolation before work
  • Maintain safe working distances
  • Report safety concerns immediately

Fault Location Process:

Systematic Approach:
  1. Arrive at fault location safely
  2. Assess site conditions and safety
  3. Set up fault locator equipment
  4. Follow systematic location procedures
  5. Document findings and location details
  6. Verify fault clearance methods
  7. Coordinate restoration with operations

Using the Fault Locator Device:

Equipment Operation:
  • Ensure device calibration is current
  • Follow manufacturer operating procedures
  • Take multiple readings for accuracy
  • Document equipment readings
  • Report equipment issues immediately

5. Progress Reporting

Field Status Updates:

Real-Time Updates:
  1. From field, access
    Field Update
  2. Select the fault being worked
  3. Choose update type:
    • Progress: Work in progress update
    • Located: Fault successfully located
    • Need Help: Request assistance
  4. Add detailed notes about:
    • Current status
    • Issues encountered
    • Estimated completion time
    • Safety concerns
  5. Submit update

Completing Fault Location:

Marking as Located:
  1. Click Located for completed fault
  2. Provide detailed location information:
    • Exact fault location (GPS coordinates if available)
    • Type of fault found
    • Clearance method used
    • Customer restoration status
    • Any follow-up required
  3. Confirm customer supply restoration
  4. Submit completion report

Requesting Assistance:

When to Request Help:
  • Complex fault requiring additional expertise
  • Equipment failure or malfunction
  • Safety concerns or hazardous conditions
  • Access issues (locked gates, dangerous terrain)
  • Customer liaison difficulties
How to Request Help:
  1. In field update, select "Need Help"
  2. Describe specific assistance needed
  3. Indicate urgency level
  4. Provide contact information
  5. System notifies depot foreperson automatically

7. Safety Procedures

Electrical Safety Requirements:

  • PPE: Appropriate for voltage level being worked
  • Isolation: Verify system isolation before contact
  • Testing: Test before touch procedures
  • Clearances: Maintain safe working distances
  • Communication: Clear communication with control room

Voltage-Specific Procedures:

Low Voltage (0.4kV):
  • Standard electrical PPE
  • Verify isolation before work
  • Test equipment before contact
Medium Voltage (11kV-33kV):
  • Enhanced PPE requirements
  • Minimum approach distances
  • Specialized testing equipment
  • Operations room coordination
High Voltage (66kV+):
  • Maximum safety precautions
  • Specialized training required
  • Extended clearance distances
  • Additional supervision may be required
Emergency Procedures:
  • Electrical accident response protocol
  • Emergency contact numbers
  • First aid procedures
  • Incident reporting requirements

8. Troubleshooting

Common Field Issues:

Issue: Cannot access fault location
Solution:
  • Contact property owner for access
  • Coordinate with depot for key arrangements
  • Report access issues in field update
  • Request assistance from foreperson if needed
Issue: Fault locator device malfunction
Solution:
  • Check device calibration and setup
  • Verify connection points and leads
  • Report equipment issues immediately
  • Request backup device if available
Issue: Complex fault requiring additional expertise
Solution:
  • Document findings thoroughly
  • Use "Need Help" feature to request assistance
  • Continue with safe investigation methods
  • Wait for expert guidance before proceeding
Issue: Cannot update system from field
Solution:
  • Check mobile device connectivity
  • Try switching between WiFi and cellular data
  • Use simple fault list view for better mobile performance
  • Contact support if persistent issues
