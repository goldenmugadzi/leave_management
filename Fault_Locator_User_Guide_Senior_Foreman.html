1. Role Overview & Permissions

Senior Foreman Authority: You have the highest level of access in the fault locator system with full management capabilities across all depots and regions.

Your Key Responsibilities:

  • Strategic fault assignment across all depots
  • Team deployment and resource allocation
  • Device management and assignment
  • Performance monitoring and optimization
  • Role assignment and user management
  • Cross-depot priority management

Access Permissions:

  • ✅ View all faults across all depots
  • ✅ Assign teams to any depot
  • ✅ Manage all fault locator devices
  • ✅ Assign roles to other users
  • ✅ Access performance analytics
  • ✅ Override depot foreperson decisions

3. Fault Management

Viewing All Faults:

Navigate to:
Dashboard → Fault Overview
  • View all faults across all depots
  • Filter by priority, status, voltage, depot
  • Sort by date, priority, clients affected

Advanced Fault Assignment:

Step 1: Go to
Advanced Fault Assignment
Step 2: Use filters to select faults:
  • Select depot(s)
  • Choose priority level
  • Filter by voltage level
Step 3: Bulk assign faults:
  • Select multiple faults using checkboxes
  • Choose available team from dropdown
  • Click Assign Selected Faults
Priority Guidelines:
  • VVIP Faults: Absolute priority - assign immediately
  • High Voltage (132kV+): Critical priority
  • High Client Impact (500+): Elevated priority
  • Critical Priority (Level 4): Same-day assignment

Priority Management:

Changing Fault Priority:
  1. Navigate to fault details
  2. Click Change Priority
  3. Select new priority level
  4. Add justification notes
  5. Save changes

4. Team Management

Team Overview:

Access:
Team & Depot Management → Team Overview
  • View all teams system-wide
  • See current deployments
  • Check team availability
  • Monitor workload distribution

Deploying Teams:

Quick Deploy (Recommended):
  1. From dashboard, find "Quick Actions" section
  2. Click Quick Deploy Team
  3. Select team from dropdown
  4. Choose depot with priority information
  5. Confirm deployment
Advanced Deploy:
  1. Go to
    Deploy Team
  2. Select team (shows device status)
  3. View depot priority information
  4. Choose optimal depot based on:
    • Number of pending faults
    • Voltage levels involved
    • Client impact numbers
    • Urgency indicators
  5. Deploy with notes

Team Recall:

Recalling Teams:
  1. Navigate to
    Team Overview
  2. Find deployed team
  3. Click Recall Team
  4. Choose recall options:
    • Immediate recall
    • Force recall (if active assignments)
    • Schedule reassignment after current work
  5. Add recall notes
  6. Confirm recall

Creating New Teams:

Team Creation:
  1. Go to
    Team Management → Create Team
  2. Enter team name
  3. Assign team leader
  4. Add team members
  5. Save team
  6. Assign device to team

5. Device Management

Device Overview:

Access:
Device & Team Management → Device List
  • View all fault locator devices
  • Check device status and availability
  • See current assignments
  • Monitor maintenance schedules

Device Assignment:

Quick Assign Device:
  1. Use Quick Assign Device from dashboard
  2. Select available device
  3. Choose team without device
  4. Confirm assignment
Advanced Assignment:
  1. Go to
    Assign Device to Team
  2. Select device (check maintenance status)
  3. Choose team
  4. Add assignment notes
  5. Confirm assignment

Device Status Management:

Status Options:
  • Available: Ready for assignment
  • Assigned: Currently assigned to team
  • Maintenance: Under repair/service
  • Retired: Out of service
Important: Teams cannot be deployed without working devices. Always ensure device status is "Available" or "Assigned" before team deployment.

8. Troubleshooting

Common Issues & Solutions:

Issue: Cannot assign team to depot
Solution:
  • Check if team has working device assigned
  • Verify team is not already deployed
  • Ensure device status is "Available" or "Assigned"
Issue: Team not appearing in assignment list
Solution:
  • Confirm team has device assignment
  • Check device status
  • Verify team members are assigned
Issue: Cannot see all faults
Solution:
  • Verify your Senior Foreman role assignment
  • Check system permissions
  • Clear browser cache and retry
