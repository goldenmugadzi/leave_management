1. Role Overview & Depot Responsibilities

Your Depot Authority: You have full management authority over fault location activities within your assigned depot area.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Manage fault assignments within your depot
  • Coordinate with teams deployed to your area
  • Verify completed fault locations
  • Set fault priorities for your depot
  • Monitor local team performance
  • Communicate with senior management

Access Permissions:

  • ✅ View all faults at your depot
  • ✅ Assign teams to faults in your area
  • ✅ Set priority levels for depot faults
  • ✅ Verify fault clearances
  • ✅ Access depot performance reports
  • ✅ Manage teams deployed to your depot
  • ❌ Cannot assign teams to other depots
  • ❌ Cannot access other depot's faults

3. Depot Fault Management

Viewing Depot Faults:

Access:
Dashboard → Fault List
  • See all faults reported at your depot
  • Filter by status (Requested, Assigned, Located)
  • Sort by priority, date, voltage level
  • View fault technical details
Depot-Specific View: You only see faults reported for your assigned depot. This ensures focused management of your area's issues.

Assigning Faults to Teams:

Simple Assignment:
  1. From fault list, click Assign next to fault
  2. Select available team from dropdown
  3. Verify team has working device
  4. Add assignment notes if needed
  5. Click Assign Fault
Bulk Assignment:
  1. Go to
    Advanced Fault Assignment
  2. Select multiple faults using checkboxes
  3. Choose team with capacity
  4. Assign all selected faults at once

Setting Fault Priorities:

Priority Guidelines for Your Depot:
  1. Click Change Priority on fault
  2. Consider local factors:
    • Number of customers affected
    • Voltage level impact
    • Available backfeed options
    • Local VIP customers
  3. Set appropriate priority level
  4. Add justification notes
  5. Save changes
Priority Levels:
  • Critical (4): Immediate assignment required
  • High (3): Assign within 2 hours
  • Medium (2): Assign within 4 hours
  • Low (1): Assign within 8 hours

4. Team Coordination

Managing Teams at Your Depot:

Team Overview:
  1. Navigate to
    Team Overview
  2. View teams deployed to your depot
  3. Check team status and current assignments
  4. Monitor team workload

Team Communication:

Coordinating with Teams:
  • Teams receive automatic notifications when assigned faults
  • You can add specific instructions in assignment notes
  • Monitor team progress through fault status updates
  • Communicate directly with team leaders as needed

Workload Management:

Optimizing Team Efficiency:
  1. Monitor active assignments per team
  2. Balance workload across available teams
  3. Consider geographic proximity when assigning
  4. Account for fault complexity and priority
Best Practice: Limit each team to 3-4 active assignments to maintain quality and response time.

5. Fault Verification Process

Verification Queue:

Access:
Dashboard → Verification Queue
  • View faults marked as "Located" by teams
  • Review team reports and location details
  • Verify fault clearance completion

Verification Process:

Step-by-Step Verification:
  1. Open fault marked as "Located"
  2. Review team leader's notes and location details
  3. Check if fault has been properly cleared
  4. Verify customer supply restoration
  5. Add your verification notes
  6. Mark as "Verified by Foreperson"
If Issues Found:
  1. Add detailed notes about concerns
  2. Contact team leader for clarification
  3. Request additional work if needed
  4. Do not verify until satisfied with completion
Verification Standards:
  • Fault must be completely located
  • Customer supply must be restored
  • Safety procedures must be followed
  • Documentation must be complete

8. Troubleshooting

Common Issues & Solutions:

Issue: Cannot see faults from other depots
Solution: This is normal - you only see faults for your assigned depot. Contact Senior Foreman for cross-depot visibility if needed.
Issue: No teams available for assignment
Solution:
  • Check if teams are deployed to your depot
  • Contact Senior Foreman to request team deployment
  • Consider adjusting fault priorities based on available resources
Issue: Cannot assign team to fault
Solution:
  • Verify team has working device
  • Check if team is already at capacity
  • Ensure team is deployed to your depot
Issue: Fault verification not working
Solution:
  • Ensure fault status is "Located" before verifying
  • Check that team has provided location details
  • Verify you have foreperson role for the depot
